Real engineer.
Real results.

I'm Lee Hildebrandt — a full stack software engineer based in Cape Town, South Africa. By day I work on enterprise-scale banking systems. By night (and weekends), I build digital products for local businesses through Mr. H Digital. As a Cape Town web developer, I help businesses stand out online with custom solutions.
