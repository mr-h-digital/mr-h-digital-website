A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Clarity,
from the start.

Because the details matter here, too.

What exactly is AudEx?

AudEx stands for Audit Excellence. It's a mobile-first web app by Mr. H Digital for structured housekeeping audits. It brings together checklists, notes, photo evidence, weighted scoring, analytics and PDF reports for environments such as clinics, hospitals and retirement villages.

Is AudEx only for WPC or one particular business?

No. AudEx is an independent product by Mr. H Digital for facility operators, housekeeping managers, independent auditors and cleaning contractors in healthcare and senior living. You do not need to work for WPC to use the software. WPC labels in the app identify the existing checklist or scoring methodology, not the product's ownership or a universal industry standard. Reduced operational checklists select from that source material. Confirm the checklist's suitability, applicable requirements and any permissions needed for your intended use before adopting it.

Do I need to download an app?

AudEx runs in a web browser. It's also an installable progressive web app (PWA), so supported devices can add it to the home screen. Load it online first to cache the app before working offline. Installation options vary by browser and device.

What happens when there's no internet?

With a current subscription entitlement, you can edit offline for up to seven days after the last online refresh, never beyond your trial or paid expiry. After that, downloaded records remain readable and exportable, and drafts are preserved until access is refreshed or renewed. Finalised audits sync when connected and entitled; drafts remain device-local. Browser storage is not a backup: don't clear it before unsent work has synced or been exported.

Can I use AudEx across my devices?

With a connected account, finalised audits, sites and training records can sync across devices signed in to that account. Drafts remain device-local, and there is no inter-account sharing. Use your own account on each device; do not share sign-in details with colleagues. Mr. H Digital manages the hosted service configuration.

Does Professional include Admin access or a team dashboard?

No. Professional increases the active-site and photo-storage allowances for one person. Admin and Owner are separately assigned platform-administration roles, not paid-plan benefits. They do not unlock other users' audit records or billing. Shared workspaces, team seats and cross-account reporting are not included.

Do I need to register as an Owner to use AudEx?

No. Customers use ordinary Auditor accounts. The Owner is the single platform-administration authority, not each facility's proprietor. You cannot purchase or select Owner access during signup. An Admin is appointed by that platform Owner.

Which checklist will I use?

During onboarding, confirm the checklist available in your deployed app and its suitability for your facility and services. Reduced operational housekeeping reviews are not independently certified compliance standards. Select every additional service present, review the check count before starting and use the full baseline when your organisation requires a comprehensive review. Existing audits retain the checklist they started with.

Can I share the findings?

Yes. AudEx generates PDF reports on your device, including audit details and captured photos. For offline reports, remote images must have been downloaded while online. Missing uncached photos are flagged rather than silently omitted.

Does an AudEx score certify regulatory compliance?

No. AudEx helps you record and assess housekeeping against its configured checklist and scoring rules. It is not a regulatory certification or a substitute for clinical, infection-control or legal judgement. Discuss the checklist's suitability for your setting before rollout.

How do I get access, and when do I pay?

Start with an access request. Mr. H Digital will confirm availability, suitability and account-setup instructions, including any trial or payment arrangements, before activation. Public self-service signup and checkout are not yet available. Planned individual prices are R199/month for Solo and R399/month for Professional, or R1,990 and R3,990 billed annually. The planned 14-day trial includes one active site and 100 MB of photo storage, with no card required, starting after email verification. Sending an enquiry or trying this website's illustrative preview does not create an account, start a trial or take payment.
