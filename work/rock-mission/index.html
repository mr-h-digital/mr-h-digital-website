01 / THE OPPORTUNITY

One organisation.
More than one audience.

A ministry's public presence, its apparel store, its children's content, its discipleship resources and its accounting work all have different jobs to do. A single generic website cannot express all of those needs equally well.

The work brings those needs into a family of dedicated experiences. Here, “ecosystem” describes their shared organisational purpose and public connections; it does not imply shared accounts or automatic data integration.

02 / BRAND ARCHITECTURE IN PRACTICE

A family.
Not five copies.

Mr. H Digital was responsible for the branding as well as the digital design and development of these projects. Each identity speaks to its audience: the ministry's bold public presence, Kingdom Drip's apparel expression, Rockidz's playful world and The Armory's focused learning experience.

The result is a useful example of designing across different contexts without asking every product to look or behave identically.
