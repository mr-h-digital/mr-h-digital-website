01 / THE EVERYDAY PROBLEM

The work after
the audit.

Housekeeping audits involve more than ticking boxes: recording observations, keeping evidence, applying scoring rules and explaining the findings. Paper, disconnected photos and separate reports make that a fragmented experience.

AudEx brings those activities into a single mobile-first tool, designed around completing the audit where the work happens.

02 / IDENTITY MEETS USABILITY

Recognisable.
Purposeful.
Built for the hand.

The brand

Mr. H Digital created the AudEx identity as well as the product. The mark, teal and amber palette and Audit Excellence name establish a distinct product language, carried into the app and the dedicated marketing website.

The experience

Phone-first checklists, notes and photo evidence for failed or partial items keep the audit process together. Drafts save on the device, so the work can continue without a connection once the app has been loaded online.

The bigger picture

Weighted and versioned scoring rules keep results explainable. Analytics help surface trends and recurring failures, while on-device PDF generation creates shareable reports.

03 / A COMPLETE PRODUCT EXPRESSION

The tool.
And the story around it.

The dedicated AudEx product website introduces the independent Mr. H Digital app through an interactive illustrative checklist, a dashboard showcase and practical explanations of how the product works.

Connected accounts can sync finalised audits, sites and training records across the same account's devices. Drafts remain device-local; individual plans do not include shared team workspaces or cross-user reporting. Customers use the hosted app rather than configuring a server, and browser storage is not a backup.

AudEx supports housekeeping assessment against configured rules; it is not a regulatory certification or a substitute for professional judgement. WPC-labelled methodology retains its provenance, separate from the independent product identity.
